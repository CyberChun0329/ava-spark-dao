# Selected B1 run-04 readbacks

[The JSON](data/b1-run-04-provenance.json) copies selected decoded event and state fields from the archived synthetic local run. It adds the recorded transaction senders for the approval, refund and filings, and the recorded signer fields for each material payment and verification. The payment executor is the same synthetic address in the payment event and saved signer field; the allocator is the approver in the second approval event. The two filings have the same recorded sender. These are the archive's own readbacks, not independently recovered transaction signatures. The material verifier's recorded signer differs from the payment executor. The addresses are synthetic test identities, not real participants.

The second payment uses the same obligation and request 2 recorded in the approval. Its transfer and payment event share a transaction hash and amount 4; the independent verification is a later transaction. Request creation, allocation, consumption and completion are separate recorded events, with consumption, completion and App approval in the same transaction. The approval readback says paid 0 at approval. Receipt 1 and receipt 2 are filed separately after verification; the refund credits the original internal account, rather than transferring cash out of the vault. Coordinates and snapshots are included for comparison with the [earlier selected ledger](data/b1-run-04.json).

The archive identifies chain 31337, local Anvil and Forge 1.5.1, Node 24.12.0, Solc 0.8.26 and the Cancun profile. Its recorded runtime resolution loaded the workspace SDK and local-service modules. Their archived package metadata says `0.1.0`; this alone does not establish that they match a later published preview package. The JSON includes the archived App and workflow IDs, the eight addresses and runtime code hashes read at deployment, and the frozen module, source, lockfile and deployment artifact digests relevant to this record. A digest identifies the archived input or artifact; it does not independently prove a deployment or reproduce the original SDK execution. The complete saved result and Core approval readback have the digests shown in the JSON; the latter matches `nextApprovalChain` in the former.

The archive lacks the original signed transaction bytes and independent third-party RPC attestations. Accordingly, sender and signer attribution and deployment bindings remain claims of the archived run. The original local chain is unavailable. Block and log coordinates are preserved, while the selected archive has no genesis or parent headers for independent chain verification. These selected readbacks do not prove a real bank transfer, reproduce all of the SDK runtime, or turn the separate published contract-level replay into the historical run. Raw signing material, calldata and full node logs are excluded.
