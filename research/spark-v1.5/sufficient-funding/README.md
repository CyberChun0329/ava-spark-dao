# Sufficient-funding receipt-timing control

This is a selected historical **offline Forge** test, not the B1 SDK run, a bank record, or a test of the current public checkout. The synthetic fixture adds 6 test units after a first actual payment of 2, giving both branches the same snapshot: cash 153, base reserves 141, first receipt unverified, and a second approval for 4. The snapshot is restored between branches. The first receipt is verified at 1000044 in the early branch and 1000046 in the late branch; in both, the same approved obligation pays 4 at 1000045 to the same second holder and the second receipt is verified at 1000047. The test asserts recipient balances 2 and 4, pool cash 149, base reserves 137, a separate refund reserve of 120, and another credit balance of 17. No filing or refund is performed.

[The added fixture](fixture.diff) preserves the original test code apart from normalized diff headers. [The prefix excerpt](prefix-excerpt.sol.txt) shows the M3 preparation and verification helpers; it is not a complete standalone fixture. [Recorded cuts](observations.tsv) transcribe the original `FundedReceiptTiming` events; [selected trace lines](trace-events.txt) preserve their original text and line numbers. Stage 0's proposed additional protection is read before payment while its 4-unit specific reservation remains. After consuming the reservation, the payment calls `requireAfterOutflow`. Its `requiredBeyondLessonHold` read returns 6 early and 8 late. Added to the base of 137, the respective funding floors are 143 and 145 against post-payment cash of 149. The late branch is therefore not represented by the earlier prospective value of 6. After both verifications current protection is 137 + 6 = 143 in both branches. Amounts are mock-token minimum units.
