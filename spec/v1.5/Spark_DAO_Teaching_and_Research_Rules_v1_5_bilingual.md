# Spark DAO 教学与教研管理办法
**Spark DAO Teaching and Course Materials Policy**

**研究候选 v1.5 · Research candidate v1.5**

供制度与形式化研究使用。中英文如有歧义，以英文为准。/ For institutional and formal study. The English text governs if the two versions differ.

## 1　适用范围和分工 / Scope and responsibilities

**1.1　适用范围**　本办法适用于 Spark DAO 安排的单人课和多人课，涵盖授课安排、学费结算、补救，以及与这些课次相关的教学材料使用和分成。学费按课次和名额登记。其他独立服务另行登记，按各自约定办理。学生、教师、教研贡献者和课程协调员应遵守本办法及事先确认的课程或材料约定。

**Scope.** This policy applies to one-to-one and group lessons arranged by Spark DAO. It covers lesson arrangements, fees and remedies, as well as the use of course materials in those lessons and the related revenue-sharing arrangements. Record lesson fees by lesson and place. Other independent services are recorded separately and handled under their own terms. Students, tutors, materials contributors and course coordinators must follow this policy and the terms agreed in advance for the lesson or materials.

**1.2　协调员职责与回避**　每堂课有一名协调员，负责排课、核实出勤与授课情况、办理结算。任何经办人，如果该项决定会直接影响其本人的应收款、应付款、责任或收款时间，应告知相关各方并回避该项决定。核心合伙人应指定无该项利害关系的人办理。回避的人仍可报告事实和提交材料。

**Coordinator duties and conflicts of interest.** Each lesson has a coordinator who schedules it, checks attendance and delivery, and handles settlement. Anyone handling a matter must disclose the conflict to the parties and step aside from the decision if it would directly affect an amount they are owed, an amount they owe, their liability or when they can be paid. The core partners must appoint someone with no such interest to handle it. A person who steps aside may still report facts and submit evidence.

**1.3　教师合作与接课**　本办法下，教师以独立教学服务提供方的身份与课程服务方合作，双方签订教学服务协议。双方在接课前约定服务内容、课酬、付款期限和各自承担的责任。本办法中的课酬指按教学服务协议支付的服务费。

教师自行决定是否接课，可以为其他机构提供服务。在约定的教学内容和服务要求内，讲授方式由教师决定。接下的课应按约完成。需要改期、取消或交接时，应联系协调员。

**Independent tutors and accepting lessons.** Under this policy, tutors work as independent teaching service providers and sign a teaching services agreement with the course provider. Before the tutor accepts a lesson, they agree the service, the tutor fee, the payment deadline and each party’s responsibilities. Tutor fees under this policy are payments for teaching services under that agreement.

Tutors may decide whether to accept a lesson and may provide services to other organisations. They choose how to teach within the agreed content and service requirements. Once a tutor accepts a lesson, they must complete it as agreed. If the lesson needs to be rescheduled, cancelled or handed over, they must contact the coordinator.

**1.4　课程服务方、客户和学生**　课程确认单应列明负责开课、收款、补救和退费的课程服务方及其联系方式，并分别列明订约客户、指定付款人、上课学生、缴纳本课学费的余额账户和学生的通知方式。几个角色可以由同一人担任。

确认单上列明的指定付款人可以代表该名额确认改期、补救课和换师安排。安排所需的其他同意齐备后，付款人的确认即为该名额的同意，无须学生再次确认。教务应按约通知学生并留存记录。临时代付的人未被列为指定付款人的，代付款本身不授予确认权限。

**Course provider, customer and student.** The course confirmation form must name the course provider responsible for teaching arrangements, fee collection, remedies and refunds, and give its contact details. It must also identify the contracting customer, designated payer, student, credit account used to pay for the lesson and how notices are sent to the student. One person may fill more than one of these roles.

The designated payer on the form may confirm rescheduling, remedial lessons and tutor replacement arrangements for that place. Once any other necessary consents are in place, the payer’s confirmation is enough for that place. No further confirmation from the student is required. The academic team must notify the student as agreed and keep a record. Paying on someone else’s behalf without being named as the designated payer does not confer authority to confirm arrangements.

## 2　报名、上课和改期 / Booking, attendance and changes

**2.1　课程确认单**　师生作出承诺前，协调员应提供课程确认单及其引用的约定。确认服务方和联系人、各方身份和确认权限、上课时间、教学内容、学费、退改办法，以及补偿、退款和投诉的办理期限。多人课写明名册锁定时间。教师事先确认本课课酬和付款期限。上课及各项处理的实际情况在发生后记录。

**Course confirmation form.** Before students and tutors commit, the coordinator provides the form and the terms it refers to. Confirm the provider and contact details, the parties and their authority, the lesson time and content, the fee, cancellation and rescheduling terms, and the deadlines for compensation, refunds and complaints. For group lessons, state when the roster is fixed. The tutor agrees the fee and payment deadline in advance. Record what actually happens during and after the lesson as it occurs.

**2.2　取消或改期**　需要取消或改期，请联系协调员。单人课的客户可在确认单约定的时限内提前取消或依约改期。已确认的团课，只要还有一名学生按原安排参加且课程仍可按约举行，就照常开课。其他学生不能单独取消名额，缺席按第 3.1 款处理。全班各名额均同意改期的，可在约定时限内与教师和课程服务方确认新的具体日期和时间。确认和通知按第 1.4 款办理。
如果各方均无过错而课程不能照原安排举行，由协调员联系各方另约。到确认单所定期限仍未接受新的安排，客户可取消受影响课次，并按第 4.3 款退回未提供教学对应的学费；完全未授课的，退足该课已扣学费。其他未约定的情况先协商。协商不成且无法按原安排上课的，按第 6.1 款处理。
**Cancelling or rescheduling.** Contact the coordinator to cancel or change a lesson. A one-to-one customer may cancel in advance within the time agreed on the form or reschedule under the agreed terms. A confirmed group lesson goes ahead if at least one student is still taking it as booked and it can proceed as agreed. Other students cannot cancel their places individually and clause 3.1 applies if they are absent. If every place in the group agrees to reschedule, confirm a specific new date and time with the tutor and provider within the agreed window. Confirm the change and notify students under clause 1.4.
If the lesson cannot go ahead as agreed and no party is at fault, the coordinator contacts those affected to agree a new arrangement. If no new arrangement has been accepted by the deadline on the form, the customer may cancel the affected lesson and have the fee for teaching not provided restored under clause 4.3. Restore the full fee debited if none of the lesson was taught. Discuss cases the terms do not cover. If no agreement is reached and the original booking can no longer go ahead, clause 6.1 applies.
**2.3　充值与学费**　客户付款充值时，课程服务方按实收金额增加其账户余额。充值和补偿发放的余额均可用于付学费。确认名额时，从确认单列明的账户扣足该课应付学费。适用优惠足以抵完应付学费的，记录抵扣依据，作已付清处理。结算学费时记录该课实扣和实退余额。学费退回原扣款账户。获赠余额只可用于课程消费。返还充值款按该笔充值交易及其适用条款办理。退回课费或发放补偿余额，本身不产生返还充值款的权利。

**Top-ups and lesson fees.** When the customer tops up, the provider credits the amount actually received to their account. Both topped-up and compensation credit can pay for lessons. Confirm a place by debiting its full fee from the account named on the form before teaching begins. If an applicable discount covers the full fee otherwise due, record the discount and treat the fee as paid in full without a debit. For each lesson, record the amount actually debited and any credit returned. Return lesson fees to the account debited. Compensation credit can only be spent on lessons. Any return of top-up funds is handled against that top-up transaction under its applicable terms. Returning lesson credit or granting compensation credit does not itself create a right to a return of top-up funds.

**2.4　课后确认**　教师向协调员报告授课情况。学生应确认自己是否出勤、收到了哪些约定服务、哪些未收到。协调员应将出勤和服务反馈分别记录。出勤确认本身不表示接受全部服务。学生未回复的，协调员应联系学生核实。对授课有异议的，学生可以按第 6.1 款反映。

**After the lesson.** The tutor reports to the coordinator on how the lesson went. Each student must confirm whether they attended and which agreed services they did and did not receive. The coordinator records attendance and feedback on the services separately. Confirmation of attendance alone does not mean the student has accepted all the services. If a student does not reply, the coordinator must contact them to check. Students can raise a concern about the teaching under clause 6.1.

**2.5　开课条件与有效上课时间**　课程服务方在确认排课前核对约定的开班条件。开课前须核对本课各名额均已按第 2.3 款付清学费，并按约通知师生。至少一名学生按约参加时，已确认的团课依第 2.2 款照常举行。课程服务方若在开课条件未满足时安排授课，仍须对客户负责，并依约结算已核实的教学；有争议的按第 6.1 款处理。

确认单所列的约定开课时间是有效上课时间。各方在授课前同意改期的，记录新时间及生效时间，以新时间计算尚未开始的课程期限。已经开始计算的投诉、退款或付款期限，不因改期重新起算。

**Requirements for starting a lesson and the agreed start time.** The provider checks the agreed conditions for forming the class before confirming the booking. Before teaching starts, verify that the fee for each place has been paid in full under clause 2.3 and notify students and the tutor as agreed. A confirmed group lesson goes ahead under clause 2.2 when at least one student is taking it as booked. If the provider goes ahead before the start conditions are met, it remains responsible to the customer and settles teaching verified as delivered under the agreed terms. Deal with disputes under clause 6.1.

The agreed start time on the form is the effective time for the lesson. If the parties agree a new time before teaching starts, record the new time and when the change takes effect. Use the new time for lesson-related periods that have not started. A change does not restart a complaint, refund or payment period that has already begun.

## 3　缺席、教学问题和补偿 / Absence, teaching problems and compensation

**3.1　学生缺席**　按本款收取缺席半费前，须核实开课条件已满足、学生已按约收到通知、教学本可依约提供，且该生未出席。课程服务方过错致使学生未获服务的，按第 3.6 款处理。已依第 2.2 款取消或改期的，按取消或改期约定办理。

团课照常举行时，该生缺席的，按该席有效约定的应付学费的一半结算并退回多扣余额，教师依本课事先约定领取该席的缺席课酬。能据此办结的，无须判断缺席原因。

单人课仅因学生未到而无法举行，核实该生无正当理由、既未到场也未准备参加的，按该席有效约定的应付学费的一半结算并退回多扣余额，教师领取该席正常课酬的一半，无须再等学生确认。学生有正当理由的，按事先确认的退改约定办理。缺席半费依本款结算。

**Student absence.** Before charging a half fee under this clause, verify that the start requirements were met, the student was notified as agreed, teaching was available to them as agreed, and they did not attend. Apply clause 3.6 if a provider error caused the student to miss the service. Where the lesson was cancelled or rescheduled under clause 2.2, apply the agreed terms for that change.

If a group lesson goes ahead, charge half the fee payable under the terms in force for the absent student’s place and return any excess credit debited. Pay the tutor the absence fee agreed in advance for that place. The coordinator can settle an absence under this rule without deciding why the student missed the lesson.

If a one-to-one lesson could not go ahead solely because the student did not attend, verify whether the student was neither present nor ready to attend and had no good reason. In that case, charge half the fee payable under the terms in force for the place, return any excess credit debited and pay the tutor half the normal tutor fee for the place. No further confirmation by the student is required. Where the student had a good reason, apply the terms agreed in advance for cancellation and rescheduling. Settle half-fee absences under this clause.

**3.2　教师过错**　教师未能按约授课，或发生严重教学问题时，协调员应向教师和受影响的学生核实情况，并让教师说明。认定教师过错时，应说明哪项已约定的服务未履行，或发生了什么可具体说明的严重事件、影响了谁。教师依第 1.3 款自行选择教法，单凭当事人不喜欢该教法不能认定过错。

认定教师过错后，对学费已付清、学生也按约出席或准备参加的受影响名额，依第 3.5 款分别办理补偿或退款。

发生教师过错的原课，扣减该教师当时约定的全部正常课酬，实付课酬为零，包括该课原本应付的缺席课酬。该教师此后为 Spark DAO 实际完成的第一堂按约本应有课酬的课，按第 3.4 款结算。客户的补偿与这两次教师课酬分别办理。

**Tutor fault.** If a tutor fails to teach as agreed or a serious problem arises, the coordinator must check what happened with the tutor and affected students and give the tutor a chance to respond. A finding of fault must say which agreed service was not provided or describe the serious incident and whom it affected. Tutors choose their teaching methods under clause 1.3. Disliking the method alone does not establish fault.

Once tutor fault is found, handle compensation or refunds under clause 3.5 for each affected place if its fee has been paid in full and the student attended or was ready to attend as agreed.

Deduct the full normal tutor fee agreed for the original lesson in which the tutor was at fault. No tutor pay is due for that lesson, including any absence fee that would otherwise be payable. Apply clause 3.4 to the first lesson after the original lesson that the tutor actually completes for Spark DAO and would otherwise be paid for. Handle the customer’s compensation and the tutor’s pay for the two lessons separately.

**3.3　多人课的过错**　教师过错只影响个别学生的，就受影响名额办理客户补偿；影响整班的，就符合第 3.2 款条件的名额分别办理。其他名额的客户学费仍按各自情况结算。查明教师当时无法向某生提供约定服务的，依第 6.3 款更正该生原有的缺席结算。

**Tutor fault in a group lesson.** If tutor fault affects only particular students, handle customer compensation for those places. If it affects the class as a whole, handle compensation separately for the places that qualify under clause 3.2. Settle customer fees for the other places on their own facts. If it turns out that the tutor could not provide the agreed service to a student, correct any previous absence settlement for that student under clause 6.3.

**3.4　下一次授课的课酬**　从出错的原课往后，在过错教师为 Spark DAO 实际完成的第一堂按约本应有课酬的课结算时，从该堂课的应付课酬中扣减一笔固定金额。扣减额等于发生过失的原课为该教师约定的正常课酬的一半。针对同一教师的同一原课，只从下一堂课的合计应付课酬扣减一次。扣减后的课酬按该堂课的付款条件办理。下一堂课可以是不同课程、不同课酬，也可以是不同学生，扣减额均以原课为准。该堂课的应付课酬不足以扣足的，记录未扣差额，按教学服务协议处理。客户另约其他教师的，原课扣减仍只适用于过错教师。其他教师按其课酬约定及本人适用的扣减结算。课程服务方分别记录教师课酬的扣减和客户补偿。已支付而须更正的课酬按第 6.3 款处理。

**Tutor pay for the next lesson.** Deduct a fixed amount when settling the first lesson after the original lesson that the tutor at fault actually completes for Spark DAO and would otherwise be paid for. Set the deduction at half the normal tutor fee agreed for the original lesson in which the fault occurred. Apply it once for that tutor and original lesson to the total fee payable for the next lesson. Pay the resulting amount under the payment conditions for that next lesson. The next lesson may be a different course, carry a different tutor fee or be taught to different students. The original lesson determines the deduction in each case. If the fee payable for the next lesson is too low to cover the deduction, record the unapplied amount and deal with it under the teaching services agreement. If the customer books with a different tutor, the deduction from the original case still applies to the tutor at fault. Settle the other tutor’s fee under their own terms and any deductions that apply to them. Record the tutor’s deductions separately from the customer’s compensation. Correct payments already made under clause 6.3.

**3.5　半价补偿和消费余额**　教师过错适用第 3.2 款，或课程服务方过错适用第 3.6 款时，订约客户可以接受本款的标准补偿。统一按事先为受影响名额约定的原课正常学费计算，半价补偿为该金额的一半，另发的消费余额为该金额的全额。后来更换课程或教师时，两项金额仍按原课确定。原课已向该生提供部分或全部教学的，将固定的半价补偿用于减免原课当时的应付学费，最多减免至零。已多扣的余额按第 4.3 款退回。属于教师过错的，即使客户尚未选择整套补偿，也应先办理原课减费和退款。客户有权要求更多退款的，依第 3.6 款处理。完全未向该生提供原课教学的，直接免收原课学费并按第 4.3 款退回已扣余额。在原课完全未授课的情形下，客户选择继续接受补偿的，可在之后选报的一节 Spark DAO 课程上享受固定优惠。优惠额为上述固定的半价补偿金额。将固定优惠用于后来所选课程时，最多抵至该课的应付学费。无论优惠用于原课还是后来所选课程，超出可抵学费的部分依适用约定处理。半价优惠可供客户报名时使用，即已兑现该项优惠。客户何时选课，由其自行决定。

客户接受上述半价补偿后，课程服务方再向其账户发放一笔消费余额，金额等于上述原课正常学费。消费余额可抵扣 Spark DAO 不同类型的课程，不可提现。仅接受退款结案的，不产生这笔消费余额。确认单记录客户接受半价补偿的时间、优惠实际用在哪一课及抵扣金额、未抵金额、应退及实退学费，以及消费余额实际到账和可用的时间。余额实际到账且可用于报名时，才算完成发放；客户何时使用余额由其自行决定。

客户用优惠或余额报名时，按所选课程正常订课。先计算该课优惠后的应付学费，再按第 2.3 款从账户扣款；退改和缺席依该课的约定办理。课程服务方应按期履行对客户的补偿和退款义务。

另行约定以授课补救的，须确定具体日期和时间，由指定付款人按第 1.4 款确认，教务按约通知学生。没有确定具体时间的，视为尚未约定该补救课。要延展已约定的补救期限，受影响各方或其有权代表须在到期前另行同意新的具体截止时间，并记录在确认单上。仅另约上课时间不延展期限；已经产生的退款照原期限办理。

**Half-price compensation and course credit.** For tutor fault under clause 3.2 or provider fault under clause 3.6, the contracting customer may accept the standard compensation in this clause. Use the normal fee agreed in advance for the affected place in the original lesson. The half-price benefit is half that fee and the additional course credit is the full amount of that fee. Both amounts stay the same if the customer changes the course or tutor, including when the later lesson has a different price. If the student received some or all of the original teaching, apply the fixed half-price benefit to the original fee otherwise due, reducing it to no less than zero. Under clause 4.3, restore any credit debited above the resulting fee. For tutor fault, make this reduction and return the excess even if the customer has yet to choose the compensation package. Apply clause 3.6 to any entitlement to a larger refund. If the student received none of the original teaching, waive the original fee and return the credit already debited under clause 4.3. If the customer chooses in that case to continue with compensation, they can apply a fixed discount to one later Spark DAO lesson of their choice. Apply the fixed half-price benefit as the discount. Apply it up to the fee payable for the selected lesson. Whether the benefit applies to the original lesson or to a later lesson, deal with any amount above the fee it can cover under the applicable terms. The provider delivers this part of the compensation by making the discount available for booking. The customer decides when to choose a lesson.

Once the customer accepts that half-price compensation, the provider must also add course credit to their account equal to the full normal fee for the affected place in the original lesson. The customer can spend the credit on different types of Spark DAO lessons. The credit cannot be withdrawn as cash. A refund taken in settlement of the case on its own does not earn course credit. Record when the customer accepted the half-price compensation, the lesson and amount to which the discount was applied, any unapplied amount, amounts due back and actually refunded, and when the credit was added and became available. Issuing the credit is complete when it is in the account and can be used to book a lesson. The customer decides when to spend it.

A customer using a discount or credit books the selected lesson in the ordinary way. Apply the discount to work out the fee due, then debit the account under clause 2.3. Changes and absences follow the terms of that lesson. The provider must deliver any compensation and refunds owed to the customer on time.

If the parties separately agree to remedial teaching, they must specify a date and time. The designated payer confirms under clause 1.4 and the academic team notifies the student as agreed. Until a specific time is set, that remedial lesson has not been agreed. To extend an agreed remedy deadline, those affected or their authorised representatives must separately agree to a new, specific deadline before the current one expires and record it on the form. Booking a different lesson time alone does not extend the deadline. Pay any refund already due by its original deadline.

**3.6　服务方过错及退款**　因课程服务方排课、通知或办理课程中的过错，学生未获得约定服务的，订约客户可选择退款，也可另约补救。因该过错未获约定教学的，还可选择第 3.5 款的标准补偿。教师过错事项按第 3.2、3.5 款办理；需要退款结案的，适用本款的退款和结案规则。

在确认单上记明客户选择、适用名额、补偿或补救涵盖的服务，以及约定的兑现期限。客户接受的补偿或补救按约完整兑现后，其涵盖的服务结清。接受后改选退款，或就已补足的服务另行退款，按适用约定和法律办理。

客户有效选择对尚未补足的原课服务退款，或这部分服务到确认单所定期限仍未约成补偿或其他补救的，应从约定学费中减去这部分服务所对应的费用。按减免后的金额结算应收学费，并按第 4.3 款办理超收部分的退款。在这些退款情形中，如果原课完全未向该生提供，且没有任何原课服务已通过客户接受并按约完整兑现的补救结清，应免收该课次学费、退足已扣学费余额。客户已接受且课程服务方仍应兑现的补偿或补救，若服务方明确无法兑现，或到期仍未按约完整兑现，即使原课或补救已部分提供，也应免收该课次学费、退足已扣学费余额。全额退款按第 4.3 款扣除已经有效退还的款项。

客户有效选择直接退款的，收到要求即按约开始办理。客户尚未选择退款、有关服务又未补足，到接受补救的期限仍未约成补偿或补救的，从期限届满时起算该笔退款的办理期。已接受的补偿或补救无法完整兑现时，应补退的款项从服务方确认无法兑现或约定兑现期限届满时起算，以先发生的为准。已经应退的款项仍按原期限办理。适用法律规定更早期限的，从其规定。教师已经按约履行的服务，按教学服务协议和第 3.2、3.4 款另行结算。

**全额退款结案。** 退足该席已扣的学费余额、免除仍未收取的费用后，结清该项尚未履行的补救和未使用的有关优惠、补偿余额。核对该课已退回的余额，依第 4.3 款只退尚欠的部分。确认单区分全退结案和免收原课学费后继续补偿。教师课酬和教研分成等按各自条款另行结算。另有适用约定或法律规定的，从其规定。

**Provider fault and refunds.** If the provider makes an error in arranging or administering the lesson or notifying the student and the student misses an agreed service as a result, the contracting customer may choose a refund or agree to another remedy. If that error caused the student to miss agreed teaching, the customer may also choose the standard compensation in clause 3.5. Handle tutor fault under clauses 3.2 and 3.5. If the case is to be settled by refund, apply the refund and closure rules in this clause.

Record the customer’s choice, the affected place, what the compensation or other remedy covers, and its agreed delivery deadline. Once the accepted compensation or remedy is delivered in full as agreed, the service it covers is settled. Follow the applicable terms and law if the customer wishes to switch to a refund after acceptance or if a refund is separately due for services already made good.

If the customer validly chooses a refund for original services still outstanding, or no compensation or other remedy has been agreed for those services by the deadline on the form, reduce the agreed fee by the amount attributable to those services. Settle the fee due at that reduced amount and refund any overpayment under clause 4.3. Where a refund is due on either basis, waive the lesson fee and restore the full credit debited for the original lesson if the student received none of the original lesson and no part of the original services has been settled by a remedy the customer accepted and the provider delivered in full as agreed. For compensation or another remedy the customer accepted and that is still owed, waive the lesson fee and restore the full credit debited for the original lesson if the provider confirms it cannot deliver the remedy or if the agreed deadline passes before it is delivered in full. This applies even if the original lesson or the remedy was partly provided. Deduct any valid prior refunds under clause 4.3.

When the customer validly chooses a direct refund, the refund period starts on receipt of the request. If the customer has not elected a refund and no compensation or remedy for the outstanding service has been agreed by the acceptance deadline, the period for paying that refund starts when the deadline passes. If accepted compensation or another remedy cannot be delivered in full, the period for paying any additional refund starts when the provider confirms it cannot deliver or when the agreed delivery deadline passes, whichever happens first. Pay refunds already due by their original deadlines. Any earlier deadline under applicable law takes precedence. Settle teaching the tutor provided as agreed separately under the teaching services agreement and clauses 3.2 and 3.4.

**Closing a case by full refund.** Restore the full lesson fee debited for the place and waive any remaining fee to settle the outstanding remedy and any unused discount or compensation credit for it. Check what has already been returned for that lesson and restore only the outstanding amount under clause 4.3. Distinguish closure by full refund from waiving the original fee while compensation continues. Settle tutor pay, materials revenue shares and other amounts separately under the terms that apply to them. Follow any other applicable terms and law.

## 4　学费、课酬和退款 / Fees, tutor pay and refunds

**4.1　单人课结算**　符合第 2.5 款开课条件，且学生、教师和协调员均确认课程完成的，办理正常结算。没有取得所需确认的，有效上课时间满 30 天后，协调员可根据核实的情况办理。仍有争议的，按第 6 节处理。

**Settlement for one-to-one lessons.** The lesson is settled in the usual way if the requirements for starting it under clause 2.5 were met and the student, tutor and coordinator have all confirmed completion. If any required confirmation is missing, the coordinator may settle the lesson based on the facts they have verified once 30 days have elapsed from the agreed start time. Any unresolved dispute is handled under section 6.

**4.2　多人课结算**　按确认单列明的有效名册计算过半人数。名册最迟在有效上课时间锁定，锁定后记录更正按第 6.3 款办理。按约仍保留名额的学生，缺席也计入总人数。

计入确认人数的是依第 2.3 款付清本课学费、确认出勤且未被认定在本课有过错的学生。人数须严格超过有效名册的一半，有效上课时间已到，开课条件满足且教师已报告完成授课，方可办理常规结算。未达到常规条件的，有效上课时间满 30 天后，协调员可按核实的情况结算。仍有争议的按第 6 节处理。

无论按常规结算还是核实后结算，先按各名额有效的退改约定和第 3.1 至 3.3、3.6 款办理缺席和过错事项。对其余已付款且款项未被暂缓处理的名额，只有团课按有效安排实际举行、且约定服务可向该生提供时，方可按该名额优惠后的应付学费结算，无须等待该生确认出勤。按第 2.5 款在开课条件未满足时先行授课的，按已核实的实际服务和服务方应承担的对客责任办理。超过半数确认出勤不认定学生已收到约定服务。协调员仍应处理未结的投诉，暂缓的学费和相应课酬留待复核。复核认定教师过错的，按第 3 节处理。已结算的款项按第 6.3 款更正。

班级结算完成后，汇总本班课酬，按第 3.2、3.4 款处理适用的扣减，再按第 4.3 款支付。个别名额的金额被暂缓时，能独立确定并结算的其他课酬仍按期支付。

**Settlement for group lessons.** Use the effective roster specified on the form to calculate whether the majority threshold is met. Fix the roster no later than the agreed start time. Thereafter, correct roster errors under clause 6.3. Keep all students who retain their places in the total, including students who were absent.

Count students whose fees for this lesson were paid in full under clause 2.3, who confirmed attendance and who have not been found at fault for the lesson. For normal settlement, the count must exceed half the effective roster, the agreed start time must have arrived, the start requirements must have been met, and the tutor must have reported completion. If normal settlement conditions are not met, the coordinator may settle according to verified facts once 30 days have elapsed from the agreed start time. Deal with unresolved disputes under section 6.

Whether settlement proceeds normally or after verification, first apply the cancellation and rescheduling terms for each place and clauses 3.1 to 3.3 and 3.6 on absence and fault. For each other paid place whose payment is not on hold, charge the fee payable for that place after any applicable discount only if the group lesson went ahead under valid arrangements and the agreed service was available to the student. The student need not confirm attendance. If teaching started before the requirements were met under clause 2.5, deal with the amounts based on services actually verified and the provider’s obligations to the customer. A majority attendance count does not establish that students received the agreed services. The coordinator must still follow up on unresolved complaints. Hold any affected fees and corresponding tutor pay for review if a hold was imposed. If a review finds the tutor at fault, apply section 3. Correct amounts already settled under clause 6.3.

Once the group settlement is complete, add up the tutor fees for the lesson, apply any deductions under clauses 3.2 and 3.4 and pay under clause 4.3. If an amount for a particular place is on hold, pay the other tutor fees that can be settled separately when due.

**4.3　学费退款和课酬付款**　协调员按课次和名额记录应付学费、实际扣除的余额、应退及实际退回的余额、课酬和教研分成。学费退款退回本课实际扣款的账户，从核定应退额中扣除该账户就同一笔退款已实际收到且未退回的金额。转错账户另按第 6.3 款处理。正常课酬在相关款项完成结算、距有效上课时间满 30 天且已达到约定付款条件时支付。对已办的退款和课酬付款留存凭据。

**Lesson refunds and tutor pay.** The coordinator records the fee due, the credit actually debited, the credit due back and actually returned, tutor pay and materials revenue shares for each lesson and place. Return the lesson refund to the account debited for that lesson. Deduct from the refund due any credit that account has already received and not returned for the same refund. Correct transfers to the wrong account under clause 6.3. Pay normal tutor fees once the relevant amount is settled, 30 days have elapsed from the agreed start time and the agreed payment conditions are met. Keep evidence of refunds and tutor payments.

**4.4　补偿和退款的资金**　课程服务方应在师生作出承诺、扣收该课学费之前，分别核对能否负担半价补偿加消费余额、退足原课已扣的学费余额、无过错取消退回未提供服务的学费，以及其他约定的补救。按每种情形计入退款、折让、消费余额和所需的课酬、教研分成及办理费用。同一项折让或抵扣只计一次。需要退足学费的，还应计入已支付或仍应支付的教学费用。团课须一并核对不同名额可能同时出现的结果，留足其他应付款。共用费用只计一次。

学费不足以承担这些费用的，由课程服务方用自身可用资金补足。可用资金以当时可支用的余额为准。先扣除其他课程或款项已经占用的资金；同一笔钱不得同时承诺给可能并存的付款。互斥的处理结果可以共用一笔准备金。已付出但尚未收回的款项和尚未收到的赔偿款不能计入退款资金。复核后需要退款的，应核对当时可支用资金能否支付尚欠退款。已经付出的课酬不重复列为待付款。资金落实前，课程服务方不能确认该课或扣收学费。安排已生效后的资金短缺，不改变客户已取得的退款权。需要调整原安排的，应征得受影响各方同意。过错事项处理完毕后，在扣除退款并留足本课应付款和已经发放但尚未使用的消费余额所需资金的前提下，剩余学费归入服务保障储备。

**Funding compensation and refunds.** Before students or tutors commit and before debiting lesson fees, the course provider must check that it can fund each outcome separately. These include half-price compensation with course credit, restoration of the full credit debited for the original lesson, a refund for services not provided on a no-fault cancellation, and any other agreed remedy. Include refunds, discounts, course credit and any tutor pay, materials revenue shares and handling costs owed in each case. Count each discount or credit only once. For a full refund, include teaching fees already paid or still owed. For a group, check which outcomes can occur together for different places and provide for the other amounts owed. Count shared costs once.

If the lesson fees are insufficient, the course provider must cover the shortfall from its own available funds. Use only funds available to spend at the time, after allowing for amounts already committed to other lessons or payments. Do not count the same money for payments that could fall due together. Mutually exclusive outcomes may share a reserve. Do not count money already paid out but not recovered or compensation yet to be received. If a review requires a refund, check whether the funds then available cover the remaining refund. Do not count teaching fees already paid as amounts still owed. It must secure the funds before confirming the lesson or debiting students’ credit. A later shortage does not change a customer’s existing right to a refund. Any changes to agreed arrangements require the consent of those affected. Once the fault case has been dealt with, the balance of lesson fees goes into the service guarantee reserve after deducting refunds and providing for all amounts due for the lesson and course credit already issued and still available.

## 5　教学材料和教研分成 / Course materials and revenue sharing

**5.1　事先约定分成**　参与编写、更新或维护材料的人员，应事先约定材料可以怎样使用、各人享有多少份额、份额如何调整，以及分成如何计算、何时支付。课程服务方采用材料分成约定时，应在约定中列明负责对账、付款和补差的主体及联系方式。协调员继续负责核实本课用料。贡献者可按公布的条件自愿转让份额，组织可按公布的条件回购。

**Agreeing revenue shares.** Contributors who write, update or maintain materials must agree in advance how the materials may be used, each person’s share, how the shares may be adjusted, and how the revenue payments are calculated and when they are paid. When adopting materials terms, the course provider must name the party responsible for checking the accounts, making payments and paying any shortfall, and give its contact details. The coordinator remains responsible for checking use in the lesson. Contributors may choose to transfer their shares under the published terms. Spark DAO may buy back shares under the published terms.

**5.2　确认实际使用**　教师课前登记预计使用的材料，课后告诉协调员实际用了哪些。协调员根据课程记录、学生反馈或其他可核对的材料，确认本次使用后办理分成。只登记、只备课，或者因缺席而没有用到的材料，不计入本次分成。如果课只上了一部分，但确实用过材料，可以单独确认。原课和以后实际使用材料的各次课分别确认本次用料。

**Confirming use in a lesson.** Before the lesson, the tutor lists the materials they expect to use. Afterwards, the tutor tells the coordinator which materials were used. The coordinator checks the lesson record, student feedback or other evidence that can be verified, and handles revenue sharing once use in that lesson is confirmed. No revenue share is payable for materials that were only listed, only prepared or not used because someone was absent. If only part of a lesson took place, actual use in that part may be confirmed separately. Confirm use separately for the original lesson and for each later lesson in which the materials are actually used.

**5.3　计算和发放分成**　按课程和材料约定计算。分成按实际用料对应的学生名额，采用各名额约定的正常学费计算，再合计发放。个别指导中使用的材料只计相应学生；缺席而未接受该项用料教学的学生不计。用优惠或消费余额报名的课次，按该课名额的正常学费计算分成。

每个适用名额的正常学费在总基数中只计一次。该课各项材料分成合计以该课正常学费基数的 10% 为上限，并依第 4.4 款核对资金。常用组合由权益人事先议定各材料比例，使任一名额即使使用组合内所有材料，分成也合计不超过该名额正常学费的 10%。使用这类组合时，按已核实的实际用料结算。其他组合在使用前核对上限。需要调整的，先与受影响的权益人约定再使用。已成立的应付款按原约定及第 6 节办理。

实际使用或归属尚未核实的，先依第 4.4 款留出预算，核实后办理；已经确定应付或批准支付的款项如需暂缓，按第 6.2 款办理。另约授课需要使用材料的，按该课的材料约定办。

**Calculating and paying revenue shares.** Calculate the shares under the lesson and materials terms. For each place to which actual use relates, use the agreed normal fee for that place, then add the amounts together. Materials used for individual instruction count only for the students concerned. Exclude students who were absent from that part of the teaching and did not receive it. For a lesson booked using a discount or course credit, use the normal fee for the corresponding place in that lesson.

Include the normal fee for each applicable place only once in the total. Cap the combined shares for materials at 10% of that total and check funding under clause 4.4. For a standard bundle, the rights holders agree rates in advance so that the combined shares for any place cannot exceed 10% of its normal fee even if all materials in the bundle are used. Settle the shares for a standard bundle according to verified use. For other combinations, check the cap before use. If an adjustment is needed, agree it with the rights holders concerned before using the combination. Deal with established amounts under the original terms and section 6.

If actual use or entitlement has yet to be verified, provide for the amount under clause 4.4 and settle after verification. Place an amount already determined to be payable or approved for payment on hold only under clause 6.2. For teaching agreed separately, follow the applicable materials terms for that lesson.

**5.4　退出与转让**　贡献者停止参加日常工作后，只要原有份额仍有效、材料仍被使用，就可按原约定取得分成。转让份额应写明生效时间，并分别约定转让前已用、尚待核实或结算的收入，已结算但尚未领取的分成，以及以后需要补发的差额归谁。对转让前已用但尚未结算的材料，双方未另约时，按该材料在该课次首次实际使用时有效的份额确定归属。已有款项仍按适用约定支付。

**Leaving and transferring shares.** Contributors who stop taking part in day-to-day work may continue to receive payments under the original terms for as long as their shares remain valid and the materials are used. The transfer must state when it takes effect. It must also say who is entitled to income from use before the transfer that has yet to be verified or settled, payments already settled but not collected, and any later payment to make up a shortfall. For use before the transfer that has yet to be settled, the default is to apply the shares in force when the material was first used in that lesson, unless the parties agree otherwise. Existing amounts are paid under the terms that apply to them.

## 6　投诉、复核和规则调整 / Complaints, review and changes to the rules

**6.1　提出异议**　订约客户或指定付款人可以就自己负责的名额的收费、退款或补偿安排向协调员提出异议并申请复核。学生可以就自己的出勤、所获服务、补救或应付给自己的款项提出异议并申请复核。教师和主张享有相关分成的人可以就与自己有关的事项提出异议。认为自己的材料使用被漏记的，可以说明涉及的材料、课程和依据并申请核查。协调员无法联系、涉及本人利益或本人被投诉时，可以直接向备用联系人反映。课程服务方应随本办法或材料约定公布通常及备用联系方式。相关贡献者即使未收到课程确认单，也可通过这两个渠道提出异议。

接收人应记录异议，并在受理回执中告知由谁办理、最迟答复日期和逾期联系人。未收到课程确认单的贡献者也应收到这些信息。经办人应核实情况并在最迟答复期限内告知结果和理由。需裁决争议、原经办人须回避或当事人要求复核时，由核心合伙人安排无该项利害关系的人办理；复核人员不能是原决定人。未回复、拒绝确认或提出异议，本身均不足以认定过错。受理异议也不产生分成权。

需要认定缺席理由、过错或其他有争议的事实时，由有权且无该项利害关系的核心教务老师作出初次决定，并记录理由。教务老师可以请决策助手核对材料、计算款项并提出处理意见。教务老师须回避时，由核心合伙人指定其他有权且无该项利害关系的人决定。复核由有权复核的人独立决定。已经确定应办的补偿、补救和退款，按各自约定办理。

**Raising a concern.** The contracting customer or designated payer may ask the coordinator to review any fee, refund or compensation arrangement for a place they are responsible for. The student may request a review of their attendance, services received, remedies or payments owed to them. Tutors and people claiming a relevant revenue share may raise concerns about matters affecting them. A person who believes use of their materials was left off the record may ask for it to be checked, identifying the materials, the lesson and the basis for the claim. If the coordinator cannot be reached, has a personal interest or is the subject of the complaint, contact the alternative contact directly. The course provider must publish its usual and alternative contact details with this policy or the materials terms. Contributors may use either route even if they did not receive the course confirmation form.

The person receiving the concern must record it and acknowledge it with the name of the handler, the latest reply date and whom to contact if that date is missed. Contributors who did not receive the form must also be given that information. The person handling it must check the facts and give the parties the outcome and reasons by the final response deadline. If the dispute requires a decision, the original handler must step aside, or a party requests a review, the core partners must appoint someone with no such interest to handle it. The original decision-maker cannot conduct the review. A failure to reply, a refusal to confirm or a complaint alone is insufficient to establish fault. Taking a concern up for review does not establish a right to a share.

Where a decision is needed on good reason for absence, fault or other disputed facts, an authorised lead academic tutor with no interest in the matter makes the initial decision and records the reasons. The tutor may use a decision-support agent to check evidence, calculate amounts and propose an outcome. If the tutor has to step aside, the core partners appoint another authorised person with no interest in the matter. An authorised reviewer decides the review independently. Carry out compensation, other remedies and refunds already due under their respective terms.

**6.2　暂缓结算**　款项尚未付出、复核人员来不及处理时，如果异议指明了具体款项，以及若查实就会改变这笔款处理结果的事实，确认单所列且无该项利害关系的备用联系人可按事先授予的权限临时留出能单独确定的受影响金额，并立即通知相关当事人和核心合伙人。备用联系人只作登记和临时留款，不裁定争议，不得暂缓整班结算。核心合伙人应在确认单规定的临时留款期限内安排独立人员作出决定。届满仍没有正式暂缓决定的，临时留款结束，该笔款仍须满足原有付款条件。暂缓只适用于尚未执行的决定或尚未付出的款项，包括已经批准但尚未付出的款项。已支付的款项按第 6.3 款处理。

有权复核的人决定正式暂缓时，应告知当事人暂缓哪项决定或哪笔款、原因和复核时间。提出或批准原结算的人不得作正式暂缓或解除决定。只有争议确实影响整堂课怎样结算时，才可正式暂缓有关的整班结算。可单独确定的其他款项继续结算，达到各自付款条件后照常支付。

到复核日还不能决定的，应说明原因并告知下次处理日期。这项通知不改变约定的最迟复核期限。到期仍未决定的，由第 6.6 款所列接办人安排有权且无该项利害关系的人复核。需要继续暂缓的，该复核人应重新说明理由和复核日期；没有继续暂缓依据的，应明确解除。解除后按第 6.6 款办理已到期而尚未支付的款项。

**Putting settlement on hold.** Before payment, if an authorised reviewer cannot act in time, the alternative contact named on the form may use prior authorisation to set aside a separately identifiable amount only if they have no interest in the matter and the complaint identifies the payment and facts that would change how it is handled if verified. The contact must immediately notify the parties and the core partners. The contact may record the concern and set the amount aside. They cannot decide the dispute or put the whole group’s settlement on hold. The core partners must arrange for an independent person to decide within the temporary period on the form. If no formal hold is imposed by then, the temporary hold ends. The original payment conditions still apply. A hold can apply only to a decision that has yet to be carried out or an amount that has not yet been paid, including payments already approved. Deal with amounts already paid under clause 6.3.

An authorised reviewer who imposes a formal hold must tell the parties which decision or payment is on hold, why, and when it will be reviewed. Anyone who proposed or approved the original settlement cannot impose or lift a formal hold. Settlement for the group may be put on formal hold only where the dispute affects how the whole lesson must be settled. Settle the other amounts that can be determined separately and pay them once their payment conditions are met.

If no decision can be made by the review date, explain why and give the next date for dealing with the matter. This notice does not alter the final review deadline. If no decision is made by that deadline, the person taking over under clause 6.6 must arrange for an authorised reviewer with no interest in the matter to review it. If the hold still has grounds, the reviewer must give reasons and set a further review date. If it has no further grounds, the reviewer must lift it expressly. Once lifted, apply clause 6.6 to any unpaid amounts that are already due.

**6.3　更正记录和款项**　经复核需要更正的，协调员应保留原处理结果，记明更正事项，并告知当事人。涉及款项的，还应记明正确金额和实际付款。补发金额按每名应收款人、每项应付款分别核对。只有该应收款人或其约定收款人就同一事项已实际收到、且未退回的金额，才从其应收额中扣除。学费退回余额的，按第 4.3 款核对原扣款账户实际收到的金额。错付给他人的款项另行处理，不能冲减应付给正确收款人的金额，也不能以追回错付款作为补发条件。

需要追回已付款项的，按原约定及有权作出的决定处理。补发和追回均另行记录，不得动用已经确定的其他应付款。资金暂缺时，向应收款人说明尚欠金额和处理进展。涉及客户补救或退款的，课程服务方按第 3.5 或 3.6 款等适用约定办理，并按第 4.4 款核对资金。

**Correcting records and payments.** If a review finds that a correction is needed, the coordinator must keep the original outcome on record, record the correction and notify the parties. If the correction concerns money, record the correct amount and the payments actually made. Calculate any amount to be paid separately for each person and each obligation. Deduct only amounts the person or their agreed recipient actually received for that same obligation and has not returned. For lesson refunds, check the credit the original account actually received under clause 4.3. Deal with any amount paid to someone else separately. It cannot reduce what is owed to the correct recipient, and payment to them cannot depend on recovering it.

Any recovery must follow the original terms and an authorised decision. Record additional payments and recoveries separately. Do not use funds owed under other established obligations. If funds are not yet available, tell the person owed the money what is outstanding and what progress has been made. For remedies or refunds to customers, the course provider must apply the relevant terms, including clause 3.5 or 3.6 as applicable, and check funding under clause 4.4.

**6.4　规则或人员变更**　核心合伙人负责公布本办法及配套约定。收费、报酬、分成或处理权限有调整的，应公布生效日期和适用范围。已承诺的课程及已经形成的付款或补偿义务按原有约定办理。变更原安排须征得受影响各方同意。人员更换后，已经批准且仍有效的结算，可由原定人员按原条件和期限办理，第 6.6 款另有补办期限的除外。新的批准由当时有权的人作出。

**Changes to rules or personnel.** The core partners publish this policy and its supporting terms. When fees, pay, revenue shares or decision-making authority change, they must publish when the change takes effect and what it applies to. Existing lesson commitments and payment or compensation obligations remain subject to the original terms. Changes to those arrangements require the consent of everyone affected. If personnel change, the person originally designated may still carry out an approved settlement that remains valid, under its original conditions and deadline, subject to the payment window under clause 6.6. New approvals must come from the person with authority at the time.

**6.5　课程服务方的损失与教师欠款**　课程服务方先向客户履行补偿和退款。教师违约给课程服务方造成实际损失时，按教学服务协议和适用规定，由有权人员核实损失金额、原因和教师应承担的部分。确认应由教师承担的金额后，记为该教师欠课程服务方的款项，另行追偿。核算时计入已经追回的金额，避免重复计算同一项损失。教师欠款与第 3.2、3.4 款的课酬分别结算。

**Provider losses and tutor debts.** The provider fulfils its obligations to compensate or refund the customer first. If the tutor’s breach causes the provider an actual loss, the authorised person verifies the amount, the cause and the tutor’s responsibility under the teaching services agreement and applicable requirements. Record the confirmed amount owed by the tutor as a debt to the provider and deal with recovery separately. Account for amounts already recovered and avoid counting the same loss twice. Settle the debt separately from tutor pay under clauses 3.2 and 3.4.

**6.6　办理期限和接办**　课程确认单应写明各笔款何时到期、最迟何时支付，投诉最迟何时答复，以及不能按期处理时由谁接办，期限可写成日期或能明确计算的期间。第 4.1、4.2 款的 30 天是缺少常规确认时何时可结算的条件，第 4.3 款的 30 天是正常课酬最早可领取的条件。各款另按约定的最迟办理期限处理，适用法律要求更早办理的从其规定。

暂缓不使已经成立的款项消失，也不重新计算原到期日。原付款日已过的欠款，在正式暂缓解除后首次不再受任何有效暂缓时，课程服务方应按确认单的补办期限尽快支付。若同一笔尚欠款项或其中一部分，在原执行期限届满时因有效正式暂缓无法支付，且此后一直受有效暂缓覆盖，确认单的补办窗口自该款项或该部分首次不再受任何有效暂缓时开启。确认单规定的是期间的，从该时点起算；规定的是固定截止日期的，沿用该日期。此后新增的暂缓不重新开启这个窗口。实际付款时原批准仍然有效、仍覆盖该笔款且其余原付款条件满足的，可由原批准指定的人在补办窗口内执行，无须重新批准。只支付届时不受任何其他有效暂缓的部分。原批准已失效或已不覆盖该笔付款的，由当时有权的人及时补办批准。

答复或复核逾期，或者款项超过适用的付款期限仍未支付的，确认单上的接办人应接手、明确责任人，并通知当事人处理进展和新的办理日期。适用上一段补办窗口时，除非适用法律要求按更早期限办理，否则只有窗口已开启、补办期限已过且款项仍未付清，才因付款逾期接办。补办窗口和接办通知均不重置原到期日，也不延长适用的法定期限。

**Deadlines and escalation.** The form must state when each payment falls due, the latest payment date, the latest date for replying to a complaint and who takes over if a deadline is missed. Use a date or a period that can be calculated clearly. The 30 days in clauses 4.1 and 4.2 set when settlement may proceed without the usual confirmations. The 30 days in clause 4.3 set the earliest date for normal tutor pay. The applicable final deadlines still govern, subject to any earlier deadline under applicable law.

A hold neither extinguishes an established obligation nor restarts its original due date. If the original payment date has passed, the course provider must pay the outstanding amount promptly within the deadline on the form once it first becomes free of all effective holds following a formal lift. If an effective formal hold prevented payment of an outstanding amount or portion when the original execution deadline passed and the same amount or portion remained continuously subject to effective holds, the form’s post-lift window opens when that amount or portion first becomes free of all effective holds. Count a period on the form from that point. A fixed date on the form still applies. A later hold does not restart this window. The original named executor can use the original approval in that window if, at actual payment, the approval remains valid, it still covers the payment and the other original payment conditions are met. Pay only the portion not covered by another effective hold at payment. If the original approval has expired or no longer covers the payment, the person with authority at the time must arrange approval promptly.

If a reply or review is overdue, or a payment remains unpaid after its applicable deadline, the person listed for escalation must take over, name the person responsible and tell the parties what has happened and the new date for action. Where the post-lift window in the preceding paragraph applies, escalate non-payment only if the window has opened, its deadline has passed and the payment remains outstanding, unless applicable law requires action by an earlier deadline. The post-lift window and that notice leave the original due date and any applicable legal deadline in place.
